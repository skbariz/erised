## BATCH 1 — Industry/Sector Momentum (H201–H205)

**Paper:** Moskowitz & Grinblatt (1999) — Do Industries Explain Momentum?  
**Core idea:** Industry momentum is a standalone signal and a momentum amplifier.  
**Data needed:** sp500_sectors.parquet, stocks adj_close, ETF adj_close  
**Note:** H196 strips sector signal from individual momentum; here we USE sector momentum as the signal.

| H# | Name | Signal | Construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| H201 | SectorETFMomentum | ETF-level sector rotation | Rank XLP, XLU, IVE, IVW, QQQ, IWM, VNQ by 6m return; long top-3 risk-parity weighted |
| H202 | IndustryConfirmedMomentum | CS: buy momentum only in positive-trend sectors | 12-1m individual momentum, but zero weight for stocks in sectors with negative 6m return |
| H203 | SectorLeaderLongOnly | CS: allocate to stocks within top-2 sectors | Long-only, risk-parity within top-2 GICS sectors by 6m return, sector-neutral sizing |
| H204 | IndustryRelativeStrength | CS: stock return relative to own sector (short horizon) | Rank stocks by 21d return minus sector-average 21d return; sector-neutral long top quintile |
| H205 | SectorMomentumRiskParity | AA: H021 variant with sector ETF replacing SPY | H021 weights: top sector ETF (best 6m return among XLP/XLU/IVE/IVW) vs TLT, risk-parity |

**Expected behavior:**  
- Bull market: sector rotation concentrates in momentum sectors  
- 2000-2002 tech crash: sector rotation should exit tech ETFs  
- 2008: should rotate to defensive sectors or TLT  
- Low-vol 2013-2017: stable sector momentum, low turnover  

**Success criteria:** OOS Sharpe > 0.60 for CS versions, H001 corr < 0.70. ETF versions (H201, H205) compared directly to H021.  
**Falsification:** If cross-sectional versions have H001 corr > 0.85, sector momentum adds nothing beyond basic momentum.

## BATCH 2 — Asset Growth & Net Share Issuance (H221–H225 moved up)

**Papers:** Cooper, Gulen & Schill (2008); Pontiff & Woodgate (2008); Loughran & Ritter (1995); Ikenberry et al. (1995)  
**Core idea:** Firms conserving capital (low asset growth, buying back shares) outperform capital-hungry firms.  
**Data needed:** fundamentals (total_assets, shares_outstanding, operating_cf, book_equity)  
**HIGH ROI** — total_assets and shares_outstanding are available and never yet used as signals.

| H# | Name | Signal | Construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| H206 | LowAssetGrowth | YoY total_assets growth (low = better) | asset_growth = total_assets_t/total_assets_{t-1} - 1; sector-neutral long bottom quintile |
| H207 | ShareholderYield | Net share count reduction | net_issuance = shares_outstanding_t/shares_outstanding_{t-1} - 1; long stocks reducing shares |
| H208 | ConservativeQuality | Low asset growth + low issuance + high OCF | z-score composite: z(-asset_growth) + z(-net_issuance) + z(operating_cf/total_assets); sector-neutral top quintile |
| H209 | BuybackMomentum | Share reduction + price momentum | long top quintile on: z(-net_issuance) + z(12-1m momentum); both signals required positive |
| H210 | CapitalDiscipline | Asset growth + issuance + debt control | composite: -asset_growth - net_issuance - (long_term_debt_t/total_assets_t); sector-neutral |

**Expected behavior:**  
- Capital-conservative firms should outperform through all regimes  
- 2000-2002: avoid highly acquisitive tech firms  
- 2008: conservative firms fare better (less leverage-driven growth)  
- Should have low H001 correlation as it is a slow, fundamental-driven signal  

**Success criteria:** OOS Sharpe > 0.55, CI lower > 0. H001 corr < 0.60 (slow-moving fundamental signal).  
**Falsification:** S&P 500 large-caps are mature; if asset growth signal is too slow and has < 100 effective trades, it won't survive CI check.

## BATCH 3 — Accruals & Earnings Quality (H211–H215)

**Papers:** Chan, Jegadeesh & Lakonishok (1996); Cooper et al. (2008) accruals dimension  
**Core idea:** High accruals (NI >> operating cash flow) signal lower earnings quality and predict underperformance.  
**Data needed:** fundamentals (net_income, operating_cf, total_assets, gross_profit, revenue)  
**NEW** — accruals signal never tested in H001–H200 (H183 used OCF as numerator, not accruals quality).

| H# | Name | Signal | Construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| H211 | LowAccruals | Sloan (1996): NI − OCF as quality | accruals = (net_income - operating_cf)/total_assets; sector-neutral long low-accruals |
| H212 | DualMomentumEarnings | Price momentum + earnings momentum joint | long top quintile in BOTH 12-1m price rank AND NI YoY rank (AND condition, not composite) |
| H213 | EarningsQualityMomentum | Low accruals + price momentum | z(-accruals) + z(12-1m momentum); sector-neutral composite |
| H214 | GrossMarginMomentum | Gross profit margin trend | gross_profit/revenue YoY change > 0 as quality filter on momentum |
| H215 | FundamentalReversal | Price fall + fundamental stability | buy stocks with 1m return < -5% WHERE NI and OCF are positive and stable (not deteriorating) |

**Expected behavior:**  
- H211 (accruals): low accruals → real cash earnings → persistent outperformance  
- H212 (dual): requires both signals → fewer but higher-conviction trades  
- H215 (fundamental reversal): buy oversold fundamentally-sound names → low H001 corr, episodic trades  

**Success criteria:** H211 Sharpe > 0.55. H212 Sharpe > 0.65 (higher bar due to filter). H215 H001 corr < 0.30.  
**Falsification:** If accruals signal is too stale (quarterly data too infrequent) → fewer than 200 effective positions.

## BATCH 5 — Same-Month Cross-Sectional Seasonality (H221–H225)

**Paper:** Heston & Sadka (2008) — Seasonality in the Cross-Section of Stock Returns  
**Core idea:** Stocks have persistent same-calendar-month return patterns across years.  
**Data needed:** stocks adj_close (long history needed: 10+ years), sp500_members, sp500_sectors  
**NEW** — never tested at stock level in H001–H200. H023/H078 use index-level seasonal patterns.

| H# | Name | Signal | Construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| H221 | SameMonthSeasonality5Y | Avg same-month return: 5yr lookback | For each stock × month: avg adj return in this calendar month over prior 5 years; long top quintile |
| H222 | SameMonthSeasonality10Y | Avg same-month return: 10yr lookback | Same as H221 but 10yr lookback; trades off more history vs stale membership |
| H223 | SeasonalityMomentumConfirm | Seasonal + 12-1m momentum AND gate | long only when both: same-month rank top 40% AND 12-1m momentum rank top 40% |
| H224 | SeasonalityAvoidList | Avoid historically weak-month stocks | Same-month history bottom quintile → zero weight; remaining portfolio reweighted |
| H225 | SectorETFSeasonality | Sector ETF same-month rotation | Rank XLP, XLU, IVE, IVW, QQQ, IWM by avg same-month historical return; long top-3 |

**Expected behavior:**  
- H221/H222: calendar persistence in returns; small but stable signal  
- H223: confirmation gate raises hit rate; fewer trades  
- H224: avoidance is implementable even in long-only mandates  
- H225: sector ETF version is low-cost and directly comparable to H023/H078  

**Success criteria:** H221 Sharpe > 0.55. H223 Sharpe > 0.65 (filtered). H225 H001 corr < 0.40.  
**Falsification:** If same-month signal is purely data-mined → CI includes zero on out-of-sample folds.  
**Caution:** High data-mining risk; require strict walk-forward with no parameter search.

**Data needed:** fundamentals (book_equity), stocks adj_close (for price)

| H# | Name | Signal | Construction |

| H232 | ValueMomentum | Cheap + positive price momentum | z(B/M) + z(12-1m momentum); sector-neutral composite (Asness 1997) |
| H233 | ValueQualityLowAccruals | Cheap + real earnings | z(B/M) + z(-accruals) + z(OCF/total_assets); sector-neutral |

**Expected behavior:**

**Success criteria:** H232 Sharpe > 0.65 (value alone is weak in this regime; needs momentum). H235 Sharpe > 0.80.

## Global warnings for implementation

1. **PIT compliance**: all fundamentals signals use `filed_date`, not `period_end`. H180 pattern is the template.
2. **Folds 0–2 gaps**: fundamentals data may be sparse before 2000. Expect zero-fold results for early folds on fundamental-heavy strategies.
3. **Trade count artifact**: cross-sectional n_trades=1 artifact from engine (see implementation gotchas memory). True trade count = number of portfolio rebalances × avg portfolio turnover.
4. **Sector regression for H216**: sector ETF history starts 2000+; earlier folds use SPY-only regression (fallback).
5. **Asset growth & issuance (H206–H210)**: requires consecutive fundamentals observations; stocks with only 1 fundamental filing will be excluded from the signal — handle with minimum 2-filing requirement.
6. **Same-month seasonality (H221–H225)**: requires 5+ years of history for each stock×month cell; early folds will have sparse coverage.
7. **B/M ratio (H231–H235)**: price (adj_close) × shares_outstanding required to compute market cap; both are available but must be aligned on filed_date.
