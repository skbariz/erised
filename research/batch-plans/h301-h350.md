**Theme:** VRP = VIX² − annualized 21d realized SPY variance. High VRP → market compensating for equity risk → increase exposure.

| HID  | Name                     | Core Mechanism |
|------|--------------------------|----------------|
| H301 | SimpleVRPOverlay         | VRP z-score → bounded SPY scaler [0.5×, 1.5×] on H021 |
| H302 | VRPRiskParityScaled      | VRP percentile → H021 total exposure multiplier (75–125%) |
| H303 | VRPTrendConfirm          | VRP > median AND SPY > 200d MA → full H021; degraded exposure otherwise |
| H304 | VRPVolTarget             | H086 vol-scale × (1 + 0.5 × VRP_zscore); cap 1.25× |
| H305 | VRPSeasonalHybrid        | H078 framework + VRP scaler applied only in Nov–Apr H021 period |

**Data:** VIX daily (squared as variance proxy), SPY daily realized variance.
**Success criteria:** Sharpe ≥ 0.85 for at least 3 of 5; MaxDD improvement vs H021 baseline.
