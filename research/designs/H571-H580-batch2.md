# H571–H580: Set8 Batch 2 — Pool Expansion and New Asset Classes

## Context

H561 established the optimal lev-Sharpe configuration: 55-strategy pool (37 AA + 18 CS),
target_vol=5%, cap=3.0x, lookback=20d. Champion Sharpe 1.921, MaxDD -7.61%.

Batch 2 asks: can we crack Sharpe 2.0 by expanding the pool with new asset classes (QQQ, IWM, VNQ)
and new cross-sectional signals (PEAD proxy, 3-factor residuals, quality+momentum weekly)?
The individual strategies test each signal in isolation; H579/H580 test whether the SLSQP optimizer
finds value in these additions.

---

## H571: QQQTLTRiskParity

**Signal:** QQQ (Nasdaq 100) / TLT inverse-vol risk parity. Same mechanism as H021 but substituting
SPY with QQQ. QQQ available from 1999-03 — folds 0-1 are sparse, fold 2+ full data.

**Why it should work:** QQQ is higher-beta than SPY, so the inverse-vol pairing with TLT produces
a different exposure than H021. The risk parity mechanism is proven; the question is whether QQQ's
distinct sector composition (tech-heavy) provides diversification vs SPY-based strategies.

**Expected behavior:** Higher returns in tech bull regimes (1999, 2013-2017, 2020-2021), worse in
tech crashes (2000-2002, 2022). The TLT hedge dampens this asymmetry.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.51, CI lower > 0, Max DD < -40%. Expected to be standalone-kept
but not a MDR pool must-have — value is as a diversifier in the ensemble context.

**Falsified if:** Sharpe below H021 (0.695 OOS training run) due to tech concentration dominating.

---

## H572: IWMTLTRiskParity

**Signal:** IWM (Russell 2000 small-cap) / TLT inverse-vol risk parity. IWM from 2000-05.

**Why it should work:** Small-cap stocks have a different business cycle sensitivity than large-cap SPY.
IWM/TLT parity may capture different crisis behavior — small caps often lead recoveries but also
lead downturns, creating distinct timing vs H021.

**Success criteria:** Same as H571. Correlation with H021 is the key question — if H001 corr < 0.50,
it has genuine ensemble value.

**Falsified if:** IWM/TLT parity essentially replicates H021 (corr > 0.80).

---

## H573: QQQIWMTLTRiskParity

**Signal:** Three-asset risk parity: QQQ + IWM + TLT. Graceful degradation when either equity ETF
is unavailable (uses available subset).

**Why it should work:** Adds size (IWM) and sector (QQQ) diversification within the equity sleeve
while TLT provides the bond anchor. Three-way parity may be more stable than 2-asset parity.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.60 (above SPY). If MaxDD < H021's -18.41%, the three-asset
structure is doing meaningful work.

**Falsified if:** Adding QQQ makes the drawdown worse than H021 due to tech crashes.

---

## H574: QuarterlyNIMomentum (PEAD proxy)

**Signal:** Cross-sectional. Each quarter, rank stocks by delta(NI)/TA (quarterly net income change
scaled by total assets, positive changes only). Long top quintile, equal-weight, quarterly rebalance.

**Why it should work:** Post-earnings announcement drift (PEAD) is one of the most replicated
anomalies in finance. Stocks with positive earnings surprises continue to drift up 1-3 quarters.
Using NI change (not surprise per se, but a proxy) in a cross-sectional rank should capture this.

**Fundamental data note:** EDGAR available from ~2009; folds 0-5 (1998-2010) will be zero or
near-zero. This is expected and will likely push Sharpe below 1.0 on the 12-fold OOS window.

**Expected behavior:** Positive but not high Sharpe (0.5-0.8) due to EDGAR gap in early folds.
Folds 6-11 (2010-2022) should show stronger alpha.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.51, CI lower > 0. Keep if it adds to MDR pool.

**Falsified if:** Signal flat/negative even in folds 6-11, suggesting NI momentum is not a viable
signal in this cross-sectional setting.

## H575: ThreeFactorDailyReversal

**Signal:** Cross-sectional. Daily reversal using market-adjusted (3-factor) residuals.
Compute market, size (IWM-SPY), value (IVE-IVW) factor loadings via 60d rolling OLS.
Daily residual = actual return - factor model prediction.
Long top decile (most negative residual = most oversold), short bottom decile. Dollar-neutral.

**Why it should work:** H191 (PCA residuals) and H192 (PCA OU mean reversion) together take 12.9%
in H559. The 3-factor model provides an economic grounding for the residual computation — the
factors (market, size, value) are well-known, theoretically motivated, and don't require
train-window PCA fitting. This reduces overfitting risk vs PCA.

**Expected behavior:** Similar to H191/H192 (reversal strategies tend to be zero-corr with H001).
The L/S structure should produce near-zero H001 correlation.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.51, CI lower > 0, H001 corr < +0.20.

**Falsified if:** Underperforms H191/H192 on Sharpe (0.83+) due to factor misspecification.

## H576: QualityMomentumWeeklyRotation

**Signal:** Cross-sectional two-stage. Monthly quality filter (top 40% by GP/A); within quality
universe, select top-20 stocks by 21-day return. Equal-weight, 5-day (weekly) rebalance.

**Why it should work:** Quality filters reduce exposure to deteriorating companies. The weekly
momentum exploits short-horizon drift that monthly strategies miss. H185 (weekly reversal) and
H293 (cost-aware monthly momentum) are both kept; this combines the complementary ideas.

**Expected behavior:** Higher turnover than H265 (quarterly quality), lower than pure daily
reversal strategies. Target Sharpe 0.70-0.90.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.60, CI lower > 0.

**Falsified if:** Weekly rebalancing of a quality+momentum signal produces similar Sharpe to
monthly (H265: 0.952) but with worse MaxDD, indicating the extra turnover is not worth the cost.

## H577: SectorMomentumVolTarget

**Signal:** Multi-asset. Universe: XLP, XLU, IVE, IVW, QQQ, IWM, VNQ (7 ETFs).
Rank by 6-month momentum, hold top-3 risk-parity weighted. Vol-target total exposure to 12%.

**Why it should work:** Sector rotation with risk parity weighting — a natural extension of H021.
The vol-targeting overlay is proven to improve Sharpe and MaxDD simultaneously (H086 lesson).
Holding only top-3 concentrates the momentum signal.

**Expected behavior:** Higher CAGR than H021 in trending markets, similar MaxDD due to vol-target.
Limited by VNQ data availability (2004-09) creating early-fold gaps.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.70, CI lower > 0.

**Falsified if:** The 7-ETF sector universe doesn't outperform a simpler SPY/TLT parity once
costs are accounted for.

---

## H578: VNQTLTRiskParity

**Signal:** VNQ (REIT) / TLT inverse-vol risk parity. VNQ from 2004-09 → folds 0-3 mostly cash.

**Why it should work:** REITs have historically acted as a distinct asset class — correlated with
equities in calm markets but with different duration sensitivity (they're rate-sensitive). The TLT
pairing provides a natural hedge since REITs typically suffer when rates rise.

**Expected behavior:** Weaker than H021 standalone (late data start, concentrated risk), but may
add value as a pool component for its distinct correlation structure.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > 0.51, CI lower > 0.

**Falsified if:** REIT/TLT parity is essentially long-duration correlation with H021 (corr > 0.80).

---

## H579: ExpandedPoolLevSharpe

**Signal:** Full pool expansion: H559's 55-strategy pool + all 8 new strategies (H571-H578) =
63-strategy pool. Lev-Sharpe SLSQP with H561 optimal params: tv=5%, cap=3.0x, lb=20d.
MDR warm-start.

**Why it should work:** The SLSQP optimizer will assign zero weight to strategies that don't
add marginal Sharpe to the leveraged portfolio. If even one of H571-H578 provides an
orthogonal return stream, the optimizer can exploit it. The optimizer has succeeded with
focused pools (H516: 1.577) and large pools (H561: 1.921) — 63 strategies is within range.

**Expected behavior:** If QQQ/IWM or the L/S CS signals add diversification, Sharpe 1.9-2.0+.
If the new strategies are dominated by the existing pool, result matches H561.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe > H561 (1.921), CI lower > 1.5. New strategies should have
non-zero weight in the solution.

**Falsified if:** Optimizer gives zero weight to all H571-H578 (new strategies add nothing),
or Sharpe declines vs H561 (spurious correlations degrade the base pool).

---

## H580: QQQIWMAugmentedPool

**Signal:** H559 55-strategy pool + H571/H572/H573 QQQ/IWM AA strategies only = 58-strategy pool.
Same lev-Sharpe SLSQP params as H561 and H579.

**Why it matters:** Isolates the QQQ/IWM contribution from the CS signal additions (H574-H576)
and sector strategies (H577-H578). If H579 > H580 by > 0.03, the CS signals (H574-H576) or
SectorMomentum (H577) are driving the improvement; if H579 ≈ H580, the QQQ/IWM component
alone is sufficient for any gains.

**Success criteria:** Sharpe ≥ H561 (1.921). If QQQ/IWM strategies receive non-trivial weight
(>5% combined), the new asset class adds genuine portfolio value.

**Falsified if:** QQQ/IWM strategies receive <2% combined weight, meaning the optimizer
rejects them as dominated by the existing pool.
